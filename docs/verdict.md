## Context the verdict does not capture

- **H4 compares unequal alert rates.** The original DEWS score's development threshold did not transfer: on held-out data it raised 36.9 false alerts per 100 clean batches, against a budget of 5. The primary system raised 1.2. The DEWS score's higher warning rates come largely from alerting almost constantly. The rule was frozen without a budget condition, so the verdict stays FAIL.
- **H5 false alerts on natural streams:** system 5.8, label-trend 5.5 per 100 clean batches. Natural streams have no known shift onset, so T2 counts alerts from 5 batches before the event.
- **Gates:** Day 2 FAILED at every allowed shift strength (harmful share 0.17-0.19); proceeded at 1.5 by owner decision. Day 4 PASSED (development T1 0.43 >= 0.40).
